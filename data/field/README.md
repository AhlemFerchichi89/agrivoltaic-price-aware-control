# Field data (Seville agrivoltaic experiment)

`DLR_Seville_FigS3_digitized.xlsx` holds data digitised from the open-access supplementary
material of Fernández Solas et al. (2026), "Monitoring and assessment of elevated agrivoltaic
systems in southern Spain", Solar Energy 316, 114845 (CC BY).

* Sheet `Fig S3 digitized`: daily mean differences APV − control (mean of three plots per treatment)
  in air temperature (°C), relative humidity (percentage points) and vapour pressure (kPa), day and
  night, for the fall 2024 and spring–summer 2025 lettuce cycles. The markers were detected
  automatically after calibrating pixels to the axis grid. Accuracy is about ±0.02 °C and
  ±0.05 pp in value; dates may be shifted by up to one day. Sheet `Check (overlay)` shows the
  detected points on the original figure.
* Sheet `Table S1 growth model`: published regressions of soil cover, fresh and dry weight on
  accumulated growing degree days and daily light integral.
* Sheet `Site geometry (Fig S1)`: layout of the installation.

Lettuce yields per treatment (Table 5 of project deliverable D2.2) are in `config.yaml`.
